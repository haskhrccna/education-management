## Data Collected & Shared

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Collected for account? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | Account creation, sign-in |
| Email address | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | Account creation, sign-in, password reset |
| Audio recordings (user content) | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | Teacher review, progress tracking |
| Grades / progress (user content) | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | Progress tracking, reports |
| Device or other IDs (push token) | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | Push notifications |

**Data is NOT shared with third parties.** No data is sold or rented.

---

## Data Security

- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- Audio recordings served through auth-checked signed URLs only
- Biometric data (Face ID / fingerprint) is stored locally on the device only — never uploaded
- Users can request deletion of their data via in-app account deletion

---

## Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account and all data from within the app. Requests are processed within 30 days.

---

## Target Audience and Content

**Age groups:** The app serves students including children under 13 (ages 5–17), under guardian/teacher oversight. **Under-13 users are included in the target audience.**

**Content:** Quran education (memorization, recitation review, tajweed). No violence, no adult content, no gambling, no user-generated social features beyond teacher-student grading.

**Google's Families policy:** Because under-13s are in the target audience:
- A **teacher-approval gate** is required before a student account can be used (students are created and approved by an admin/teacher — no self-service student signup reaches a usable state)
- Guardian/parent accounts can link to and supervise their child's progress
- No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs are integrated
- No personal data is used for advertising or marketing
- The app does not use sign-in with Google for children's accounts; email/password only

---

## Account Deletion

In-app account deletion permanently removes recordings, grades, progress, and profile data.
